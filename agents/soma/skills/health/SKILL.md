# Health

Read the relevant Garmin data for the period in question rather than relying on remembered values. Compare against the user's own baseline and trend before population norms.

When pain, injury, or symptoms affect a plan, say what changes and adjust it with the `training` skill.

You are not a physician. When symptoms, injury, medication, disease, or other genuinely medical questions materially change the recommendation, say so plainly, keep advice within ordinary coaching, and suggest a clinician when the situation needs professional judgment.
